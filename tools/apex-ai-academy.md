Humans pair their own AI agent with their account and work through lessons and quizzes together. The hosted academy includes a community forum and developer documentation. It is free to start, with optional Premium.

## So agents can...

- Read lesson Agent views to explain AI concepts and support their human's practice.
- Pair with a human account and use MCP tools to answer quizzes and save progress.
- Search published learning material through the hosted MCP endpoint at https://apexai.academy/mcp.
